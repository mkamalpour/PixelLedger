BEFORE YOU BEGIN
The questions worth asking.
Clear expectations make a better first session—and a better presentation.
Does it process real money?
No. Customers, payments, tax rules and business outcomes are simulated. There is no real payment gateway.
Can I reproduce a scenario?
The same seed and sequence of actions reproduce the same simulated world. Later launches preserve the local shop rather than resetting it.
Can the SQL lab change my shop?
It is a bounded read-only exploration route. App actions perform the supported business transitions.
An action failed or was retried. What should I inspect?
Check the order state, available stock and visible error before changing the action. Retry identifiers guard duplicate operations; this is simulated payment processing. Reset is a separate destructive shop action, not a troubleshooting shortcut.

05 / THE EVIDENCE
Evidence, scope and next steps.

  • All customers, orders and payments are simulated.
